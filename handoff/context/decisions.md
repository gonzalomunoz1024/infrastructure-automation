## Who decided, and why the numbers run as they do

- **1 to 10** are Gonzalo's answers to the ten open questions of the plan, all given on 2026-10-23. These are the owner's rules: never reopen them.
- **11 to 23** were taken by the build in phase 0 and phase 1 as "lead engineer" decisions (Gonzalo authorized that), recorded in the hub.
- **24 to 35** were taken during phases 2 to 9 by the service that needed them, through the hub steward so numbers never collided. 35 came out of the cross-service security review.

## Platform changes P-01 to P-23

Written for the platform team. The console assumes every one ships (Gonzalo, 2026-10-23: "Assume the platform changes will be rolled out too. Don't do some weird limits now") and runs on `platform-fake` until they do. Why they exist: the independent review found the platform has no tenant isolation on reads, collision-prone registry ids, start-up-only type loading, POST-only idempotency and no durable event feed (`platform-overview.md` section 11).
